Kinetic Forces

KineticForces computes the neoclassical toroidal viscosity (NTV) of a perturbed equilibrium: the toroidal torque and the kinetic energy that a non-axisymmetric field exerts on the plasma through the drift-kinetic response of its particles. It implements the PENTRC formulation of Park et al. (2009), Logan et al. (2013) and Park and Logan (2017).

What it computes

A non-axisymmetric perturbation $\delta B$ breaks the toroidal symmetry that conserves the canonical angular momentum of each particle. The resulting non-ambipolar radial transport, dominated by trapped particles whose bounce and precession motion resonates with the E×B rotation, carries toroidal momentum out of the plasma: the NTV torque $T_\phi$, which brakes or spins up the rotation, together with a kinetic contribution $\delta W_k$ to the perturbed energy that stabilizes or destabilizes the response. Both are halves of one complex quantity, $T = T_\phi + 2in\,\delta W_k$, and the module integrates it over the plasma for one or more torque methods.

The torque enters the pipeline in two ways:

Governing equations

The torque is a sum over species of three nested integrals, over flux $\psi$, over the pitch variable $\Lambda$ separating trapped from passing particles and over the normalized energy $x = \varepsilon/T$, with a sum over the bounce harmonics $\ell$ up to bounce_harmonic_max. The integrand is the bounce-averaged perturbation of the field strength $\langle \delta B_\parallel / B \rangle_\ell$, evaluated from the displacement $\xi$ of the perturbed equilibrium, weighted by the Maxwellian and by the resonance factor $1/(i\Omega(x) - \nu)$: the energy integral is Eq. 8 of Logan et al. (2013) and the torque and its normalization Eq. 19, $\operatorname{Im} T = 2n\,\delta W_k$. The resonance frequency $\Omega(x)$, and the diamagnetic frequencies that relate the E×B rotation $\omega_E$ of the kinetic file to the toroidal rotation, are given with their sign conventions under KineticForces rotation conventions. The collision operator is selected by nutype: "harmonic" (the default), "krook", "small" or "zero" (the collisionless limit), with the collision frequencies computed from the density and temperature profiles; f0type selects the equilibrium distribution, "maxwellian" (the default), "park" or "cgl". The kinetic file may also supply the Coulomb logarithm and collision rates lnLambda, nu_i and nu_e, which then replace the collision model of the NTV calculation (Logan et al. (2013), Eq. 6) that otherwise computes them from $n$ and $T$. Each is optional and must be positive; they are interpolated, and extended past the table's ψ range, in log space, so supply them over the whole range. A supplied nu_i is the first main ion's rate, and the other ions and the impurity keep the model's $(Z_s/Z_1)^2\sqrt{m_1/m_s}$ ratio to it. They feed only the NTV calculation (the tearing layers' resistivity keeps its lnLambda_form closure), the density and temperature scaling factors do not rescale them, and collisionality_factor still multiplies them.

Torque methods

Each method is one evaluation of the same integral under a different model, switched on by its <method>_flag in [KineticForces]; the first letter selects the particle population, f full, t trapped only, p passing only.

Running it

From examples/DIIID-like_ideal_example/gpec.toml, after the [PerturbedEquilibrium] section (examples/TJ_kinetic_NTV_example is the same stage on an analytic equilibrium):

[KineticForces]
bounce_harmonic_max = 6          # bounce harmonics summed over ℓ = -max:max
electron = false                 # add the electron NTV to the ion species
nutype = "harmonic"              # collision operator: "zero", "small", "krook", "harmonic"
f0type = "maxwellian"            # distribution function: "maxwellian", "park", "cgl"
atol_pitch = 1e-9                # absolute tolerance of the pitch integration
axis_validity_suppression = true # suppress the near-axis terms where the model is not valid

In a script the stage is one call on the perturbed equilibrium:

using GeneralizedPerturbedEquilibrium

kinetic = kinetic_torque(pe, KineticForcesControl(; bounce_harmonic_max=6, nutype="harmonic"))

The torque is three nested adaptive Gauss–Kronrod integrals, and each inner tolerance is derived from the outer one unless set: rtol_psi (1e-2, the primary knob; two significant figures match the model's validity), rtol_pitch (psi_tolerance_margin × rtol_psi) and rtol_x (nested_tolerance_margin × rtol_pitch). Tightening rtol_psi tightens the whole ladder; tightening an inner level alone only multiplies evaluations. The kinetic matrix build of a kinetic = "KineticForces" solve has no ψ quadrature and uses rtol_pitch (1e-3 when unset).

Profile scaling

Seven factors on KineticForcesControl scale the kinetic inputs for sensitivity studies. density_factor, temperature_factor, ExB_rotation_factor and toroidal_rotation_factor act on the profiles before any physics evaluation: the diamagnetic frequencies are formed from the unscaled profiles, $\omega_E$ is rebuilt from the scaled total rotation $\omega_\phi$ minus the scaled diamagnetic terms and then multiplied by ExB_rotation_factor, and the collision frequencies are recomputed from the scaled density and temperature. magnetic_drift_factor, collisionality_factor and divxi_factor multiply the magnetic drift, the collisionality and $\nabla\cdot\xi_\perp$ at evaluation time; use collisionality_factor to scale the collisionality alone. The four profile factors are not supported together with a multi-ion ion_species list; the three evaluation-time factors are.

Multi-ion runs

With several main-ion species the NTV is computed per species under one shared $Z_\mathrm{eff}$ and summed over the main ions, the quasineutrality-closing impurity and, with electron = true, the electrons (electron always means in addition to the ions). The sum is written to KineticForces/<method>/ as in a single-ion run and each species to KineticForces/PerSpecies/<label>/<method>/, with labels ion_z<z>_m<m>, impurity_z<z>_m<m> and electron; the summed total_torque is the exact quadrature value, the summed profiles a linear interpolation onto the union grid.

Regularization

[PerturbedEquilibrium] regularization_width multiplies $\xi^{\psi\prime}$ and $\xi^\alpha$ by $Q^2/(Q^2 + w^2)$ with $Q = m - nq$ before they drive the NTV integrand. It exists because the ideal response diverges as $1/Q$ at every rational surface, so the torque integral of an ideal solve does not converge; the self-consistent kinetic solve has no such singularity, since the finite torque moves the zeros of the kinetic coefficient off the real $\psi$ axis (Park and Logan (2017)). The default is 0; set it, typically to 0.05, only for the torque of an ideal solve, and leave it at 0 for a kinetic run, where it would suppress a finite physical response.

GeneralizedPerturbedEquilibrium.KineticForces._bounce_points_at_lambda! — Method

Bounce points of v_par(θ) = 1 − (λ/bo)·B(θ) for a trapped particle. B = bo/λ at a bounce point, so a monotone interval between stationary points of B contains one iff bo/λ lies strictly between its endpoint B values; the crossing is then solved from the cell's cubic, with hints carrying each interval's cell over from the previous λ.

Returns roots sorted ascending, as Fortran spline_roots, so the deepest-well search pairs neighbouring roots; fewer than two roots signals a degenerate λ and sends the caller to the fallback. hints is sized length(bf.theta) + 1, the extra slot being the far half of the interval that wraps through the seam.

Radial profiles of the drift-kinetic validity diagnostics, on the kinetic-profile ψ grid: psi; the thermal orbit-width scales rho_i (gyroradius √(2mT)/(Z·e·B₀)), rho_banana (q·ρ/√ε), rho_theta (poloidal gyroradius q·ρ/ε), w_potato ((q²ρ²R₀)^(1/3)); the local geometry r_minor (⟨r⟩) and d_separatrix (⟨r⟩(1) − ⟨r⟩(ψ)); the profile gradient lengths L_p and L_q (|X|·|dr/dψ|/|dX/dψ|, Inf where the profile is flat); the near-axis suppression boundary psi_c (kinetic_axis_validity_psi) with its envelope; and is_valid — true where every zero-orbit-width ordering holds at coefficient 1: max orbit width < r, ρbanana < Lp and Lq, and max(ρi, ρbanana, ρθ) < d_separatrix. Validity is diagnostic only — nothing outside the near-axis envelope is suppressed (the far edge and steep-gradient regions are flagged, not zeroed, since they can dominate the physical NTV).

GeneralizedPerturbedEquilibrium.KineticForces.tpsi! — Method
tpsi!(tpsi_var, psi, n, l, zi, mi, wdfac, divxfac, electron, method, equil, intr,
      kinetic_profiles; op_wmats=nothing, surface=nothing, kwargs...) -> nothing

Toroidal torque from nonambipolar transport at one ψ and bounce harmonic l, written to tpsi_var[]. The imaginary part carries the kinetic energy, Im(T) = 2n·δW_k. Zero for ψ > 1.

Arguments

  • tpsi_var::Ref{ComplexF64}: Output torque
  • psi::Float64: Normalized poloidal flux
  • n::Int: Toroidal mode number
  • l::Int: Bounce harmonic number
  • zi::Int: Ion charge in fundamental units (e)
  • mi::Int: Ion mass (units of proton mass)
  • wdfac::Float64: Drift factor
  • divxfac::Float64: Divergence factor
  • electron::Bool: Calculate quantities for electrons (zi,mi ignored)
  • method::String: Integration method (FCGL, *GAR, *TMM, *WMM, *KMM) where * = F,T,P for full,trapped,passing
  • equil: PlasmaEquilibrium with 2D interpolants and named profile/geometry splines
  • intr::KineticForcesInternal: Internal state with mode indexing and perturbation splines
  • kinetic_profiles::Equilibrium.KineticProfileSplines: Named kinetic-profile splines (ni, ne, Ti, Te, ωE, νi, ν_e) loaded from kinetic.dat

Optional Arguments

  • op_wmats::Array{ComplexF64,3}: Store ForceFreeStates matrix elements
  • surface: Per-surface bundle from _torque_surface, shared across the bounce harmonics of one ψ; built here when not given
  • nutype, f0type, nufac: Collision operator, equilibrium distribution and collisionality factor passed to calculate_gar (the [KineticForces] keys nutype, f0type, collisionality_factor)
